Privacy Policy for 1K CLUB

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1K CLUB respects your privacy and is designed to process your running data primarily on your device.

Health Data

1K CLUB requests read-only access to workout data from Apple HealthKit.

The app reads running workout information including:

  • Workout date and time
  • Workout duration
  • Running distance
  • Workout identifier

1K CLUB does not write, modify, or delete any data in Apple Health.

Health data is used only to provide the app's running tracking, statistics, recap, and widget features.

Data Collection

1K CLUB does not collect or transmit your personal data to the developer.

The app does not use:

  • User accounts or login systems
  • Backend servers
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Analytics SDKs
  • Tracking SDKs
  • Crash-reporting SDKs

HealthKit data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

Local Data Storage

Some information may be stored locally on your device to provide app features, including:

  • Completed running dates
  • Workout identifiers
  • Workout start times
  • Running distance and duration
  • App theme and stamp preferences
  • Notification settings
  • Widget data

The 1K CLUB Home Screen widget receives limited app data through Apple's App Group mechanism. This data remains on your device.

Photos and Sharing

When you select a photo or generate a shareable image, the content is processed on your device.

1K CLUB does not upload your photos or generated images to a developer-operated server.

If you choose to share content using the iOS system share sheet, the destination is selected and controlled by you.

Data Deletion

Because 1K CLUB does not maintain user accounts or a backend database, the developer does not retain a copy of your personal or HealthKit data.

Local app data can be removed by deleting the 1K CLUB app from your device.

Health data stored in Apple Health is managed separately through Apple's Health app and is not deleted by 1K CLUB.

HealthKit

1K CLUB's use of HealthKit data complies with Apple's HealthKit requirements.

HealthKit data is not used for advertising, marketing, tracking, or data brokerage purposes.

Children's Privacy

1K CLUB does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
